## GedCode 0.5 replaces the Ged orchestrator

GedCode 0.5 is rebuilt on a new orchestrator. The old Ged orchestrator — the PM sessions and task stages from 0.4 — is deprecated and is not part of 0.5 in that form. Planning, implementation and verification now run through the new orchestrator: a lead thread hands work to planner, implementer and verifier agents, and the verifier is never the agent that did the work.

**What you keep:** your projects and threads. They open in 0.5 as regular threads.

**What you lose:** PM history — PM sessions, tasks, stage history and gates — does not carry over and is not shown in 0.5. It is not deleted: it stays in the 0.4 database (`~/.gedcode/userdata/state.sqlite`), and reinstalling GedCode 0.4.8 shows it again.

Also new in 0.5:

- The command-line tool is `gedcode`, and project settings live in `gedcode.json`. An existing `t3.json` is no longer read; rename it to `gedcode.json`.
- New worktree branches are named `gedcode/…`.
